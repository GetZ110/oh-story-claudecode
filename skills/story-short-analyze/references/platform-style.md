## Episodic serialization norms (all platforms)

The English episodic short-fiction scene runs on **per-episode hooks**:

| Norm | Rule |
|------|------|
| Episode length | 1500-3000 words per episode; 8-15 episodes per story (a 10k-20k word arc) |
| Hook per episode | every episode opens with a micro-hook (a question, a threat, a change of state) within the first 100 words |
| Cliffhanger endings | every episode ends on an unresolved beat — a revelation, a threat, a choice, a door opening |
| Paywall placement | the strongest break (the point readers most want to flip past) sits at the episode boundary chosen for the paywall/unlock |
| Density around breaks | plot-point density steps up in the episodes before and after the paywall |
| Serialized voice | prose favors short paragraphs, mobile-first rhythm, dialogue-led scenes; the episode reads as a self-contained beat while feeding the arc |

## Core style traits per platform

| Trait | Wattpad | Inkitt | Radish / Dreame / GoodNovel / Galatea | Tapas |
|-------|---------|--------|----------------------------------------|-------|
| POV | first-person heavy; confessional voice | mixed; voice-driven | first-person dominant, direct emotion | first/third; visual-scene prose |
| Tone | slow-burn, comment-driven community warmth | twist-first, hook culture | high-stakes melodrama, fast gratification | episodic, cliffhanger-tuned |
| Episode focus | the emotional beat, the slow burn | the reveal, the premise | the cliffhanger, the comeuppance | the scene, the visual hook |
| Reading model | free, community, comments as feedback | free, algorithm-hooked | paid/free-with-ads episode unlocks | free + premium episodes |
| Distinctive element | reader comments, fan dynamics | contests, writing-community discovery | chapter-end paywall engineering, cover-driven clicks | vertical scroll, episode previews |
| Format | plain paragraphs, mobile-first | plain paragraphs | short paragraphs, heavy dialogue | short scenes, strong episode titles |

## The platform checklist during teardown

| Check | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| POV consistency | all first-person, no POV jumps? |
| Dialogue format | unified quotes, no mixed styles |
| Sentence rhythm | long/short interleaved; narration comma-joined, short sentences only at emotion beats; no all-short telegraph |
| Colloquial register | no written/translated register residue |
| Emotional rhythm | a clear emotion curve (the platform's core demand is the emotional tug) |
| Hook position | a hook in the first 100 words of each episode |
| Cliffhanger position | an unresolved beat at each episode end |
| Reversal position | the main reversal at 70-85% of the piece (platform standard for shorts) |
| Episode word counts | within 1500-3000 words; the paywall sits at the strongest break |

## Platform types and emotional patterns

| Platform type | Core emotion | Typical reversal |
|---------------|--------------|------------------|
| Radish / Dreame / GoodNovel / Galatea (paid episodic) | instant gratification + cliffhanger itch | the comeuppance lands publicly, the unlock promises more |
| Wattpad | slow-burn belonging | the confession the reader waited 20 chapters for |
| Inkitt | twist-driven curiosity | the premise reveal that re-frames the story |
| Tapas | episodic wonder | the episode-end hook that changes the scene's meaning |

## Platform style differences (comparison)

| Dimension | Paid episodic (Radish/Dreame/GoodNovel/Galatea) | Wattpad | Inkitt / Tapas |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|---------|----------------|
| POV | first-person limited | first-person limited | unrestricted |
| Length | 1500-3000 words/episode, 8-15 episodes | flexible chapters, 1500-3000 words | episodes, often shorter |
| Core sell | gratification rhythm + unlock engineering | emotional connection + community | novelty + twist |
| Dialogue style | short, functional, subtext-light | naturalistic, banter-heavy | scene-driven |
| Reader expectation | read one more episode | feel something, belong somewhere | find out what happens |
| Payment model | episode unlock / ads | free | free / premium episodes |
