# Kindle / KU Quality Rubric

## Scoring standard

| Metric | PASS standard | FAIL standard |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| KU binge-readability | Chapters read fast and flow together; scene-end pulls continue the binge | Chapters that stall, info-dump, or end on flat pauses |
| Page-turn rate | Each chapter end opens a question, threat, or promise that carries into the next | 3+ consecutive chapter ends without forward pull |
| Market fit | Genre and trope clarity: a reader who picked the category gets exactly what it promises | Genre drift, trope bait without delivery, or category mismatch |
| Series read-through | Book 1 ends with a reason to open book 2; series arc visible | Book 1 closes completely; no series hook |
| Chapter length | Chapters sized for one sitting (2k-5k words typical for KU) | 8k+ word chapters or wildly inconsistent lengths |
| Review profile | Rating + review count align with category norms | Rating without sample size, or review-flagged quality issues (grammar, unresolved threads) |
| Completion estimate | Strong opener + page-turn pacing -> estimated read-through above 40% | Weak opener or recurring drag -> below 20% |

## Usage

- story-review's story-architect perspective references this standard
- advisory only, not blocking
- Kindle / KU rewards binge-readability, page-turn rate, and market fit; read-through from book 1 to book 2-3 is the main series growth lever
