# Royal Road Quality Rubric

## Scoring standard

| Metric | PASS standard | FAIL standard |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Progression pacing | A visible progression beat (level-up, skill gain, power-up, system reward) every few chapters; arc-level gains per volume | More than 5000 words without a progression beat, or gains that never change the protagonist's options |
| Follow-through | Chapter-end hooks (question, threat, promise, decision) pull readers to the next chapter; follow-through is the visibility metric | 3+ consecutive chapters end without a reason to continue |
| Chapter-end hooks | Hooks land on action, image, or unanswered tension | Chapters end on summaries, states, or flat pauses |
| System/progression clarity | Rules and numbers are legible; choices and costs are visible | Stats/skills float without consequence; rules change ad hoc |
| Genre fit | Content matches tags (progression fantasy, litRPG, xianxia-inspired, sci-fi) | Drifts off-tag for more than 3 chapters |
| Pacing | Scenes advance plot, progression, or relationships; no filler arcs | Multi-chapter stretches without plot/progression movement |
| Completion estimate | Strong opening + steady hooks -> estimated follow-through above 30% | No hooks in the first 3 chapters or recurring drag -> below 15% |

## Usage

- story-review's story-architect perspective references this standard
- advisory only, not blocking
- Royal Road rewards progression pacing, follow-through, and chapter-end hooks
