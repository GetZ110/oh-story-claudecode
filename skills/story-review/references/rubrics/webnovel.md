# Webnovel Quality Rubric

## Scoring standard

| Metric | PASS standard | FAIL standard |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Episode hooks | Every episode ends on a reason to open the next (reversal, reveal, threat, cliff) | 3+ consecutive episodes end without a next-episode pull |
| Serialized pacing | Fast gratification: a visible payoff, reversal, or reveal every 1-2 episodes | Consecutive episodes that only extend status quo |
| Power-stone engagement | Scenes give readers moments worth supporting (comeuppance, flex, big reveal) | Long stretches with no support-worthy beat |
| Genre/tag match | Content matches the tags (trope clarity in title + opening) | Drifts off-tag for more than 3 chapters |
| Reading friction | Short paragraphs, mobile-friendly pacing, low cognitive load | Wall paragraphs, slow rhythm, dense lore dumps |
| First-chapter hook | Chapter 1 opens on conflict, stakes, or identity gap | Chapter 1 opens on weather/backstory/slow build |
| Retention estimate | Episodic hooks + anticipation management -> estimated retention above 40% | No episodic hook or recurring drag -> below 20% |

## Usage

- story-review's story-architect perspective references this standard
- advisory only, not blocking
- Webnovel (webnovel.com) rewards serialized episode hooks and power-stone engagement; freemium conversion depends on hook placement in the early episodes
