# English Platform Review Policy

Use the book's `AGENTS.md` language and market profile first, then apply the
matching platform row. This is a review layer, not a promise about acceptance
or current platform policy; verify platform changes before submission.

| Platform | Primary review focus | Packaging and metadata |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Road | progression clarity, visible advancement, chapter hooks, system consistency | title, tags, genre, content warnings, chapter cadence |
| Webnovel | fast gratification, serial escalation, power-system clarity, retention | episode hooks, title, tags, rating, content warnings |
| Wattpad | voice, relationship pull, mobile readability, comment-friendly episode rhythm | blurb, tags, cover, rating, content warnings |
| Inkitt | genre promise, opening information gap, scene hooks, feedback-test readiness | blurb, tags, cover, rating, content warnings |
| Radish / Galatea | app-first escalation, emotional cliff, paywall boundary, episode length | episode packaging, blurb, cover, mature-content disclosure |
| Dreame / GoodNovel | trope clarity, conflict velocity, romance/relationship payoff, serialization | blurb, cover, content rating, sexual/violence warnings, metadata |
| Tapas | episodic readability, accessible voice, clean section landing, binge rhythm | episode title, thumbnail/cover, tags, rating, content warnings |
| Kindle/KU | manuscript formatting, genre positioning, page turns, series read-through | front/back matter, blurb, cover, marketplace metadata, content rating |

Every platform review must explicitly check:

- content rating and mature-content warnings;
- profanity, sexual content, violence, and sensitive-topic handling;
- title, blurb, cover, tags, categories, and other required metadata;
- serial-episode versus ebook-manuscript formatting;
- US/UK spelling and market fit from the book contract.
