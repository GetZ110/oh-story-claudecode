# Progression Fantasy / Cultivation Prose Card

## Prose directive
Progression fantasy is a ledger with a heartbeat: the reader must always know the protagonist's level, what they lack, and what the next step costs. Write the numbers into the texture — a breakthrough, a scar, a resource owed — never as an abstract panel floating over the story. The prose should be direct and functional in setup passages, then open up at the peaks: breakthroughs and duels get the sensory slow-motion, everything else moves briskly. The genre voice favors clean verbs and concrete costs over ornament.

## Opening hooks
- Cut in at the gate: a sect trial, a family test, a resource stolen, a duel invitation, a realm door opening.
- First scene establishes the power ladder and where the protagonist stands on it — and why they have to climb.
- The protagonist's flaw is the engine: a broken core, a forbidden technique, a debt that must be repaid at speed.

## Conflict engine
Realm suppression + resource scarcity + position. Growth must show results rung by rung; breakthroughs carry costs and witnesses. The ladder itself is the antagonist's architecture.

## Payoff & emotion release
Release comes from over-tier wins, resources secured, realm breakthroughs, identity flips, and new-map qualifications. Every payoff leaves a higher-pressure rung exposed. The reader must feel the upgrade in the body: new senses, new weight, new fear in others' eyes.

## Dialogue & voice
Sect elders, family heads, rivals, and mentors must carry rank in their speech — no uniform hot-blooded shouting. The protagonist's lines can be short and hard, but must state a goal, a judgment, or a bottom line.

## Chapter-end hooks
Use the next trial, a dungeon gate, a rival arriving, a resource exposure, an elder's changed attitude, or a new-map qualification. The end always raises the next rung's pressure.

## Scene granularity
The texture lands on proving grounds, assessment stones, pills, technique fragments, beast corpses, sect tokens, resource ledgers, wounds, and watching disciples. Setting nouns must hang on concrete gains or dangers — never float.

## Prose landing points
Open on the proving ground, the test, the stolen resource, or the dungeon gate; conflict lands on realm suppression and resource gaps; endings land on a new trial, dungeon qualification, a rival's arrival, or an elder changing their tune.

## Early/mid/late play
- Early: press the despised opening with tests, stolen resources, family humiliation, or dungeon qualifications; show the upgrade gap clearly.
- Mid: settle around realms, pills, rankings, arenas, and dungeon gains; comeuppance must tie to resources landing.
- Late: drag grudges, sect interests, and higher powers into one settlement; end on a stronger rival, dungeon depths, or an elder's changed stance.

## Pacing density
Early rhythm runs the five-step loop: pressured, trial, evidence/resources gained, small turnaround, higher pressure. Between breakthroughs and over-tier fights there must be preparation; no cost-free consecutive upgrades.

## This-chapter tradeoffs
Pick one spine per chapter — training, resources, or combat — and let the others assist. Don't stack realms, maps, techniques, bloodlines, and factions at once; the prose must not become a setting table.

## Drift bans
No empty hot-blooded shouting; no level-table dumping; no cost-free breakthroughs; no combat without a resource chain and a goal chain underneath.

## Sample passages

> 1. The assessment stone was cold against Lin Chen's palm, and cold was all it gave. The elder's voice carried to the last row of the hall: "Qi Training, ninth grade. No marrow, no bloodline, no master's seal." The hall laughed the way halls laugh — in layers, the front row polite, the back row loud. Lin Chen bowed, and on the way down he let his sleeve catch the stone, just once, the way his grandmother had taught him. The stone flickered. Nobody saw it. The elder's eyes did, and the elder said nothing, and Lin Chen walked out with the seed of a debt already planted.

> 2. The breakthrough cost him the Qi he'd hoarded for winter, a pill his master had saved for a year, and the feeling in his left hand for six hours. When it came back, it came back with a pulse he could hear: his own blood, moving like a tide, and beneath it, the faint brass note of the formation under the sect floor. He could hear the sect. He could hear the mountain. He opened his eyes and his master was standing over him with the ledger open. "Well," the old man said. "Now you can pay rent."

> 3. The rival sect's champion had won twelve duels that season, and the crowd chanted his name like a war drum. Yan Xi stepped onto the stone with one hand behind her back. The elder of ceremonies frowned. "You'll fight as you are?" "As I am," she said. The champion swung. The crowd's roar had not yet peaked when the stone under his feet turned to water — her technique, bought with a favor, practiced until the motion was a breath. He went down with his own momentum, and she put a foot on the water and waited for the count. Twelve duels, the hall would learn, was not the same as one.
