# Vex

## Basic Information

- **Identity:** The Collector, the Ledger's enforcing spirit; the book's ink made walkable
- **Core traits:** Exact, patient, without cruelty or kindness; honors every promise the book makes, which is the whole danger
- **Current state:** Holds Corin's surety (one unearned title, since settled); witnessed the relighting and bowed once
- **Core motivation:** None. It keeps the accounts. That is its entire nature
- **Weakness:** It cannot imagine a debt that should not be collected; forgiveness is the one arithmetic it must borrow from Corin

## External Presentation

**Appearance**
Taller than any person in the district, dressed in the texture of dried ink, with no face to read and a voice that sounds like a page turning. It does not walk so much as get filed from place to place. In daylight it is a shadow with edges; at night, the ledger's own candle.

**Behavior**
- Speaks in entries: "Debt: one name. Owed: the seal. Collector: the Page-Bearer."
- Never volunteers information, never withholds a question's true answer.
- Bows exactly once in a hundred years, and the district learns the meaning by watching the barons' faces.

## Inner Analysis

**Character**
Vex is not a villain and not an ally: it is the book's enforcement clause. It honors promises exactly, which means it is the most trustworthy being in the realm and the most dangerous. Its neutrality is the story's pressure valve: the barons cannot bribe it, Corin cannot beg it, and every character must deal with the system as it is, not as they wish it were.

**Goals**
None. It enforces the Ledger's entries and waits for the pages to close.

**Secret**
It knows the torn page's contents. It has always known. It will tell the realm's next keeper when the account is ready, and not a word before.

## Speech Style

- The book's grammar, always: "An entry once written is a promise once made. A promise once made is a debt once owed. A debt once owed is mine."
- One sentence of almost-warmth, at the close of volume 1: "The account is settled, Page-Bearer. The next page is yours." It is the closest the book has come to a blessing.
