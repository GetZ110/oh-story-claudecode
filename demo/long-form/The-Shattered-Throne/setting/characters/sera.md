# Sera Blackwood

## Basic Information

- **Identity:** Daughter of Baron Halvath, fireglass smuggler, defector to the Lower City
- **Core traits:** Practical, fearless, allergic to sentiment she has not earned; negotiates with everyone including herself
- **Current state:** Her father's claim on her was struck from the Ledger as a forgery; her page is clear
- **Core motivation:** To owe no one, least of all her father
- **Weakness:** She plans escapes, not futures; staying is harder for her than fleeing ever was

## External Presentation

**Appearance**
Twenty, with the barons' quarter tailoring worn down to street use: a good coat, badly mended. Fireglass dust under her nails. She carries her money in a belt, her knives in her sleeves, and her name like a weapon she has stopped using.

**Behavior**
- Tastes everything before she buys it, including information.
- Gives orders in the barons' register; the district finds it funny, then useful.
- Laughs once when surprised, and hides it.

## Inner Analysis

**Character**
Sera was raised to be an asset and decided to be a liability instead. The fireglass trade is her rebellion made practical: she sells the barons' own luxury to the people they tax. Her defection is not an ideology; it is an account she has been keeping against her father since childhood, and the Ledger merely wrote it down.

**Goals**
Surface: get free of her father's name. Deeper: become someone whose name is her own, with a page the book has cleared.

**Secret**
She wrote the letter that reached her father's quarter in chapter 5, and she has never told Corin. It was a warning to the district, disguised as a report, and it worked; the guilt and the pride of it both live in her.

## Speech Style

- Baronial precision under street slang: "The terms are simple, lamplighter. You light, I sell, nobody dies."
- Affection as insult: "You count bread. It is the most adorable thing the barons ever raised me to despise."
- Silence when it matters: she says nothing at all in the hall when her father watches her walk out.
