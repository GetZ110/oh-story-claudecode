## Relationship Design Notes

**Corin and Mila (the moral meter):** Mila is the realm's unpaid debt made flesh, and Corin's care for her is the book's real accounting. Every time the system prices her, the reader is invited to check the sum against what she means. Her chalk wall is the district's true ledger, and the two books never quite agree.

**Corin and Sera (the held thread):** The relationship is deliberately never named. They trade, fight, and rescue each other inside the book's grammar, and the one sentence that would settle it is deferred to volume 2. Sera's open page and her father's old claim keep the thread structurally alive.

**Edric (the spent coin):** The steward is the volume's sacrifice, paid in the book's own currency: a name. His emptiness after chapter 12 is the story's quiet answer to every question about what the system costs.

**Halvath and Vex (the system's two faces):** Halvath forges entries; Vex enforces them. The baron's tragedy is that he believed his own forgery, and the Collector's neutrality is what makes the ending fair: the book does not take sides, it takes accounts.
