## Core Conflict

**Main contradiction:** The Ledger pays titles for collected oaths, and the Ash Barons rule on uncollected ones. Every title Corin earns makes the barons' hundred years of unpaid debt more visible, so the more he helps people, the more the city is endangered.

**Secondary contradictions:**
- Sera owes her father an obedience she will not pay; the Ledger keeps her entry open.
- Edric holds the Vault key but the Vault is sealed by a debt only royal blood can pay, and the last king is dead.
- The Collector, Vex, enforces the book without caring who wins.

## Character Arcs

### Corin Vale
From apprentice who lights lamps on orders, to Page-Bearer who lights them on purpose, to the boy who chooses a new kind of throne. His arc in this volume: obedience -> wonder -> duty -> cost -> choice. The memory burned in chapter 15 is the first permanent price and the moment he stops being a passenger.

### Sera Blackwood
From smuggler daughter to defector to hostage to rescuer. Her arc completes at chapter 15 (she rescues herself); her Ledger entry (the debt to her father) stays open, which is the volume's quietest thread.

### Edric Hale
The last royal steward. He gives his name in chapter 12 (the Vault's price), then his life's work in chapter 18. His death is not in this volume, but his name is spent, which is the book's version of death.

### Mila
The realm's unpaid debt made flesh. She is taken in chapter 13, recovered in chapter 15, and her presence is the moral meter of the whole siege: the city is worth saving because she is in it.
