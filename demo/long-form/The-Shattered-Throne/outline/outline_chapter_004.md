### Chapter 4: The Fireglass Girl

- Core event: Sera's secret is exposed to Corin: she is Baron Halvath's daughter, smuggling fireglass out of the barons' quarter to buy her way free, and the Ledger shows her father's claim on her as an open entry.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Opening arc, chapter 4 of 20; volume 1, unit 1.4
- Unit ID/position: Unit 4 (The Fireglass Girl) / second supporting-cast unit
- Target emotion: Intimacy and dread; the reader falls for Sera in the same scene they learn the cost of her freedom
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants to help Sera without touching her entry; his choice is to refuse the Ledger's collection order and pay the refusal in memory
- Chapter positioning: Relationship chapter with a system twist; the refusal rule is introduced through love, not through law
- Chapter structure formula: Secret shared + entry revealed + collection refused + memory burned
- Opening hook: Sera's fireglass comes from the barons' own kilns, and the barons do not export
- Payoff: The refusal rule is demonstrated: refusing a collection burns one memory, and Corin loses his mother's name for half an hour
- Forbidden early release: The barons' coup mechanics, Rook's capture, the Vault, the flame's nature
- Contract risk: The memory cost must land as grief, not as a stat penalty; keep it felt and specific

Uncollected. Collector: none."
