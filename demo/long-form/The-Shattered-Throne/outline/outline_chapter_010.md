## Chapter Outline (Chapter 10)

### Chapter 10: The Lower City Rises

- Core event: The district relights itself in public: every lamp on Flame Street and the Tallow Gate comes up at once in Corin's name, and the city sees its first light in a hundred years that the barons did not license.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Midpoint, chapter 10 of 20; volume 1, unit 2.5
- Unit ID/position: Unit 10 (The Lower City Rises) / midpoint unit, first public victory
- Target emotion: Overflowing hope with a blade of fear; the book's biggest joy so far, immediately priced
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants the district's lamps on one more night; his choice is to make it a public act, knowing the barons will answer in public
- Chapter positioning: High pressure climax of the first half; the volume's midpoint
- Chapter structure formula: Night gathering + mass lighting + witness + barons' answer
- Opening hook: The district has one night of oil and one name on everyone's lips
- Payoff: The mass relighting pays the unearned title pledge: Mila's name is held, and Corin's second page brands itself on his arm
- Forbidden early release: The flame's nature, the regent's ghost, Rook's orders, the siege
- Contract risk: The joy must be collective, not solo; the chapter is the district's, and Corin is its instrument

#### Content summary (five-part)

- Cause: Corin calls the district to the square at dusk with the week's oil in one cart and a plan in his head / - Development: Families light their lamps from a single flame; the light spreads street by street, and the district begins to sing an old song / - Turn: The Tallow Gate lantern comes up on its own, and with it every lamp the Ledger has ever recorded in the district / - Climax: The mass lighting settles the pledge: the title is earned by the act itself, and Corin's second page brands itself on his arm as Mila's name is struck from the debt list / - Ending: The city watches from every window; by morning, the barons' answer is already moving: ash guards close the district's gates
