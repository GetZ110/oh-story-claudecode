## Chapter Outline (Chapter 12)

### Chapter 12: The Steward's Price

- Core event: The chapel is sealed by a debt only royal blood can pay, and Edric pays it with the only royal thing he carries: his name, given to the book, and struck from it forever.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Rising action, chapter 12 of 20; volume 1, unit 3.2
- Unit ID/position: Unit 12 (The Steward's Price) / cost unit, emotional peak of the middle third
- Target emotion: Grief with gratitude; the book's first permanent sacrifice
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants to open the chapel without paying the price; his choice is to let Edric choose, and to witness it
- Chapter positioning: Relationship chapter; the steward's arc reaches its hinge
- Chapter structure formula: Door sealed + price named + choice made + name spent
- Opening hook: The chapel door has no lock; it has a debt, and the debt has a face
- Payoff: Edric's name enters the book, the door opens, and the flame's lamp is visible at last; Edric is no longer Edric Hale, he is only the steward
- Forbidden early release: The flame's nature, the regent's ghost, the barons' knowledge, Sera's capture
- Contract risk: The sacrifice must be chosen, not forced; Edric's agency is the chapter's whole point
