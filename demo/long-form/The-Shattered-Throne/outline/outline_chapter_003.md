## Chapter Outline (Chapter 3)

### Chapter 3: Keeper of the Lanterns

- Core event: Sera Blackwood enters the Lamp District selling fireglass, and Edric Hale, the last royal steward, recognizes the page-brand on Corin's arm and follows him home.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Opening arc, chapter 3 of 20; volume 1, unit 1.3
- Unit ID/position: Unit 3 (Keeper of the Lanterns) / first supporting-cast unit
- Target emotion: Curiosity and warmth with a cold undercurrent; the reader meets the book's two anchors and its first hunter
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants to keep his title secret; his choice is to use the power to save Mila from a beating, showing the brand to a street full of witnesses
- Chapter positioning: Relationship chapter; the cast locks in before the conflict escalates
- Chapter structure formula: Stranger arrives + brand exposed + ally found + hunter named
- Opening hook: A woman sells fireglass that burns without oil, and every lamplighter in the district wants to know her name
- Payoff: Edric's recognition of the brand gives the magic a history; Sera's fireglass gives the city a new fuel
- Forbidden early release: The barons' coup details, the Vault's location, Rook's orders, the flame's true nature
- Contract risk: Three new characters in one chapter can crowd the page; give each one a single defining action

#### Content summary (five-part)

- Cause: Sera opens her fireglass stall in the district market; the glass burns clear and steady and cheap / - Development: Corin, testing his title at dusk, lets a gust shake every lamp but his; the street notices; a patrol begins asking questions / - Turn: Mila is caught by a merchant whose goods she has been lifting; Corin steps in, and the brand on his arm shows in the light / - Climax: Edric Hale, an old man in a patched coat, takes Corin by the elbow and names the mark: "Page-Bearer. They told me you were a myth." / - Ending: Sera watches the whole scene from her stall and buys a loaf she does not need; Rook, an ash guard captain, is told of a boy whose lamp would not gutter

#### Plot arrangement (multi-line)

- Main line: Brand exposed; Edric enters the story; Rook's hunt begins / - Sub-line: Sera's fireglass thread: why does a baron's daughter sell glass in the slums? / - Event & task line: The market day; the gust test; Mila's theft caught / - Task blockers: The patrol's questions; the crowd; Corin's ignorance of what the brand means / - Relationship line: Mila's loyalty hardens; Edric's first act of stewardship; Sera's interest is commercial and personal at once / - Logic line: A title used publicly is a debt advertised, and the barons collect advertisements

#### Characters and appearance order
