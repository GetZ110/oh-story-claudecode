## Chapter Outline (Chapter 1)

### Chapter 1: A Last Candle

- Core event: Corin lights the last lamp on Flame Street and hears the Ledger of the Realm for the first time, which records his deed as a debt owed to him by the realm.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Opening arc, chapter 1 of 20; volume 1, unit 1.1
- Unit ID/position: Unit 1 (The Last Candle) / opening unit of the Lamp District thread
- Target emotion: Wonder laced with hunger; the reader should feel the city's grey poverty and then the first spark of the system
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin's only goal is to light the lamps before the flame-tax collector arrives; his choice is to light the last lamp anyway, knowing the street is scheduled to be abandoned
- Chapter positioning: Ordinary advance with a wonder beat; establishes the city, the boy, and the book in one night
- Chapter structure formula: Ritual open + small mercy + system first contact + cost hinted
- Opening hook: The lamp tax is due at midnight, and Corin has one candle left to light the whole street
- Payoff: The Ledger writes its first entry in his name; the reader learns the book pays in titles
- Forbidden early release: The nature of the First Flame, the barons' names, Sera, Edric, and the Debt of the Throne entry
- Contract risk: Do not let the wonder beat outrun the poverty; the system must feel earned, not granted

#### Content summary (five-part)

- Cause: The Ashfall has killed the city's light trade; the lamp tax is due and the Lower City cannot pay / - Development: Corin lights the lamps one by one with a single dwindling candle; the last lamp belongs to a street scheduled for abandonment / - Turn: The wind comes up and his flame holds where every other flame would gutter; something in the dark reads his name / - Climax: The Ledger appears, an account book no one else can see, and writes: "Debt recorded: the realm owes Corin Vale one light" / - Ending: Corin hides the book's existence from Master Pell and sleeps with one eye open, the page faintly warm on his skin

#### Plot arrangement (multi-line)

- Main line: Corin lights the lamps; the Ledger opens its first account / - Sub-line: Mila watches him from the shadows of a broken cart; she takes the last unlit candle / - Event & task line: The lamp-lighting ritual; the tax collector's round begins at midnight / - Task blockers: No oil, one candle, a wind that eats flame, a street that is officially dead / - Relationship line: Master Pell's weary trust; Mila's silent curiosity / - Logic line: A light paid for out of pocket creates a debt, and debts in this world are books

#### Characters and appearance order

- Appearance order: Corin Vale, Master Pell (mentioned), Mila (shadow, unnamed), the Ledger (voice)
- Relationship changes: Corin's first link to Mila forms through the candle she takes; Pell's authority is quietly cracked when Corin lights the dead street anyway
- POV & information gap: Corin's POV only; the reader knows the Ledger is real, the city does not; Mila's theft is hidden from Corin
