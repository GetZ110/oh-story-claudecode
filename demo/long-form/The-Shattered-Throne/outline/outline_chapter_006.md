## Chapter Outline (Chapter 6)

### Chapter 6: The Ash Barons

- Core event: Corin learns the barons' history from Edric and sees the cost of his title: Baron Halvath announces a new ash tax on the Lamp District, aimed at whoever lights it up.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Rising action, chapter 6 of 20; volume 1, unit 2.1
- Unit ID/position: Unit 6 (The Ash Barons) / first opposition-history unit
- Target emotion: Slow anger; the reader is taught to hate the barons in the abstract before hating them in the flesh
- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants to keep lighting lamps; his choice is to pay the new tax quietly rather than expose the book
- Chapter positioning: Ordinary advance; institutional worldbuilding with a personal sting
- Chapter structure formula: History lesson + decree + quiet resistance + thread planted
- Opening hook: The five barons rule the city the way tenants rule a house they mean to burn for the insurance
- Payoff: Corin lights the district's lamps anyway, on half the oil, and the Ledger notes the tax as a new uncollected debt against the barons
