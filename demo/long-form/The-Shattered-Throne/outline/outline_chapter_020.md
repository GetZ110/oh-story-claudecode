### Chapter 20: The Throne of Debts

- Core event: Corin walks into the Ash Court alone, answers the realm's oldest question by choosing a new kind of throne, and the Ledger closes its hundred-year account with one last entry written in a hand that is no one's.
- Target words: 2200
- Stage position: Volume close, chapter 20 of 20; volume 1, unit 4.5

- Protagonist goal/key choice: Corin wants the realm's debt answered and the barons' reign ended without a massacre; his choice is to take the throne as a keeper, not a king
