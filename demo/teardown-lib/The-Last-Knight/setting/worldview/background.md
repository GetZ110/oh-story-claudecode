# Worldview: Background of Valdris

## Core Rules

1. **The Ashfall.** For ninety years, grey ash has fallen across the kingdom of Valdris. It kills crops, blurs roads, and tastes of salt near the coast. The Coven teaches that it is a punishment for false oaths sworn by the old kingdom.
2. **The truth (as of the sample's reveals):** the Ashfall is not a punishment but a debt. Ninety years of unkept oaths have accumulated, and the land is collecting them. What the Coven calls weather, the records call an account book.
3. **Oath-magic.** Sincere oaths burn with silver light. The light scars the curse's creatures (the salt-wraiths), which are themselves uncollected oaths given shape. Every oath costs the speaker a memory; the price is paid immediately and specifically.
4. **The Long March.** The Coven's sentence for serious crimes: walk east into the Ashlands until you die. Wardens accompany the line; no prisoner returns. The March is both punishment and maintenance, because the Coven uses it to feed the Ashlands something (the records imply the truth is worse than the rumor).

## Special Settings

- **The masks.** Wardens wear smooth iron masks, so that no prisoner can identify them. Masked authority is the world's visual signature: the Coven has no faces, only ranks.
- **The records.** The Coven archives everything. Crimes, compliments, weather, and men who light up in the dark. The archive is the world's true government; the masks are its soldiers.
- **The silver light.** Rare and hunted. Men who emit it are "light-men", collected by the Vowless and paid for well. The sample does not yet reveal what the Coven does with them.

## Rule Consistency Notes

The world's rules are introduced in the same order the protagonist needs them: sentence first (records), then weather (Ashfall), then debt (the voice), then magic (silver), then cost (memory). No rule is stated before it can be demonstrated, which keeps the worldbuilding feeling discovered rather than dumped.
