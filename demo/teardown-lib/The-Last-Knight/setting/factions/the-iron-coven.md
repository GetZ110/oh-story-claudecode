# Faction: The Iron Coven

## Identity

The ruling mage guild of Valdris. They administer the Ashfall, run the Long March, control the archives, and maintain the only functioning record of oaths in the kingdom. Their symbol is a smooth iron mask, which their operatives wear so that no one can name them.

## Structure

| Layer | Role | Known members |
|---|---|---|
| The Archive | The real government: every crime, compliment, and light-man recorded | Maren (former clerk), unnamed clerks |
| The Wardens | Enforce sentences; run the Long March | The two masked wardens (chapters 1-5) |
| The Vowless | Enforcer who collects light-men | One, face never shown |
| The Mask | The leadership, never seen in the sample | Unknown |

## Methods and Ideology

The Coven rules by paperwork. Their power is not martial; it is archival. They do not need to be feared as individuals because they do not exist as individuals: every act is a form, every sentence is a record, and the masks make identity irrelevant. Their doctrine holds that the Ashfall is a punishment for false oaths, which conveniently makes the curse the people's fault and the Coven their only protection.

## The Faction's Story Function

- Provides an antagonist that can scale forever (infinite clerks, infinite records) without a single villain needing to be invented.
- Creates the book's signature texture: bureaucratic horror. The warden's compliment is scarier than any scream, because it proves the system sees everything.
- Holds the truth the protagonists need: the records of what the curse really is. The conflict is a document dispute, and the resolution will be a correction to the record.

## Internal Contradictions (plot fuel)

1. The Coven says the curse punishes false oaths; the records say the curse collects unpaid ones. The doctrine and the archive disagree, and Maren stole the evidence.
2. The Coven destroys records that would explain the curse, but the wardens honor honest theft. The institution has a conscience problem it does not admit.
3. The Coven pays well for light-men but marches light-men east. Supply and demand in the same document.

## Relationship to Other Powers

- The Dawnward Order (dissolved): the knightly order that guarded the old oaths. Its last members are marched, not executed, as if the Coven fears making them martyrs.
- The villages: taxed and marched, never governed.
- The coast: the place where records go to be burned, and where the truth was supposed to die.
