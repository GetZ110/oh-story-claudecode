# The Cheat: Oath-Silver

## What It Is

A broken knight's remnant magic. The Dawnward Order bound its knights with oaths, and Kael is the last man still carrying that binding. When he swears a sincere oath, the oath burns silver along his blade and body. The silver is the only light that scars the curse's creatures.

## Activation Rules

1. **Sincerity required.** The oath must be real and specific. Kael cannot swear to "do good"; he must swear to "walk Maren to the coast".
2. **Payment required.** Every oath costs one memory, taken immediately. The memory is specific and named: chapter 5 costs Kael his mother's name.
3. **No loopholes.** The magic treats evasion as breaking the oath, which the world implies is worse than paying.
4. **Visibility.** The light is visible to others. Roran sees it, Maren sees it, and the Coven has a price list for it.

## Power Curve (as demonstrated in the sample)

| Stage | Power | Cost | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed | Faint silver on the hands at night | None yet | 2 |
| First oath | Silver edge on the blade | Price announced (memory) | 3 |
| Combat use | Edge scars wraiths; door held all night | Mother's name | 4-5 |
| Next stage (projected) | Unknown; the sample ends here | The book implies each oath costs more | - |

## Weaknesses

1. **The price compounds.** Each oath makes the next one more expensive to justify, which is exactly how the story wants the protagonist to suffer.
2. **Sincerity is a constraint, not a resource.** Kael cannot use the power for convenience; using it means meaning it.
3. **The Coven hunts it.** Light-men are collected, and the Vowless has already been sent once.
4. **It cannot heal or protect.** The silver scars wraiths but does nothing for Roran's wound. The power is an edge, not a shield.

## Design Notes

The cheat is a character trait first and a power second: the magic only works because Kael is the kind of man who stops for a dead boy. Every display of the power is therefore a display of the personality, which is why the sample never feels like the magic is solving problems the character did not earn. When adapting this cheat, keep the rule: the power's activation condition must be a moral condition, not a mechanical one.
