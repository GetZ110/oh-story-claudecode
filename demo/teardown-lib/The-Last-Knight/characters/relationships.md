## Relationship Design Notes

**Kael - Maren (the alliance that costs):** The book builds trust through a ritual of exchange: he gives the blanket, she gives the bread, he gives an oath, she gives the truth. Every exchange raises the price of the next one. By chapter 5 the relationship is the story: the oath that binds them is the oath that costs him his mother's name, and Maren is the only person who can record what he forgets.

**Kael - Roran (the mirror):** Roran exists to show Kael his own future and then step out of it. The generation rhyme ("Put it in the record" / "The record keeps no weather") makes the mentorship feel structural rather than sentimental. His death is the book's first permanent cost and it is paid by the magic system, not by the villains.

**The Coven - everyone (the institution):** The antagonists are never individuals; they are a bureaucracy with masks. This lets the book escalate conflict (wardens -> Vowless -> the archive) without inventing new villains, because the institution has infinite reach and zero face.
