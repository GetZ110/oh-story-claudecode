# Chapter 3 Summary: The Archive Thief

- **Words:** 1,120 | **Stage position:** Opening hook, chapter 3 of 5
- **Tone tags:** tense, warm-cold alternation
- **Theme tags:** oaths, price, trust

## Plot Points

1. Maren joins the March; she is a Coven archive clerk whose brother was marched years ago [setup]
2. She shares her bread with Kael; the first warmth of the book [character bond]
3. Maren's satchel holds records meant to be burned: the curse's mechanism, partially readable [escalation]
4. At dusk the Vowless arrives, a masked enforcer sent to collect light-men [crisis]
5. Kael swears his first oath (to walk Maren to the coast); silver light runs along his blade [turn, magic]
6. The Vowless withdraws; Maren names the price: every oath costs Kael a memory [hook]

## Characters

Kael, Maren (joins), Roran (side), the Vowless (first antagonist encounter).

## Chapter-End Hook

The oath's price is stated for the first time: each sworn promise takes a memory. The reader now watches every future oath with two stakes at once.
