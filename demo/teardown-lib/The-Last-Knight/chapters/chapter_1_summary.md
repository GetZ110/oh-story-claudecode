# Chapter 1 Summary: The Last Prisoner

- **Tone tags:** grim, dry, bureaucratic
- **Theme tags:** oaths, records, mercy

## Plot Points

1. Kael sits in a cell; a clerk reads his charge as a tax list: theft from a Coven granary [setup]
2. Kael's defense: "It was winter. The granary was full." The clerk refuses to record it [setup, character]
3. Sentence: the Long March; they leave a rope as a courtesy [hook]
4. The March forms at the West Gate; the Ashfall is ninety years old; two masked wardens [world]
5. Roran, an old knight of the dissolved order, joins the line [character]
6. On the third day a young prisoner dies; Kael covers him with the stolen blanket [turn]
7. The warden calls it "the most honest theft I have ever recorded" [payoff, dry humor]
8. Maren, a Coven archive clerk, watches from the road and records Kael's name in a hidden ledger [hook]

## Characters
