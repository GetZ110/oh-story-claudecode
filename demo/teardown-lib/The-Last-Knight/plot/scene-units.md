## Unit Design Notes

- Average unit length: one page. No unit overstays its single job.
- Every unit ends with a small transaction: an exchange (bread, blanket, name, record) or a fact. This is the book's unit signature: scenes are trades, not experiences.
- The two "teaching" units (U12, and the exposition inside U5) are the only units without conflict, and both are kept under half a page. The book never lets exposition outlive its purpose.
