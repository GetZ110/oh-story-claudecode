Scattered or open threads not yet bound to a plotline. In the 5-chapter sample these are intentional open hooks, not dropped details; each has a designed recovery target.

## Loose Thread Management Notes

- No thread is introduced without a location to be recovered (chapter or arc). This is the sample's discipline: open hooks are scheduled, not abandoned.
- The dead boy's namelessness is the deliberate exception: it is a theme beat (the book is about names), and the recovery is the reader's own recognition, not a plot event.
- When adapting this book's structure, keep the same ratio: roughly one new open thread per chapter, with a named recovery target for each.
