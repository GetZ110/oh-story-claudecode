# Plot Points: The Secret Keeper

Plot points with source quotes and emotion markers. Emotion marker scale: cold / warm / dread / ache / chill.

| # | Plot point | Source anchor | Emotion marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The letter arrives under the lighthouse door, damp and ink-blurred | "as if it had swum the last mile itself" | Cold |
| 2 | Elara recognizes the handwriting; she has not forgotten it in five years | "Five years of widowhood had not taught her to forget" | Ache |
| 3 | She reads it in the lamp room, in the chair of his funeral | "the chair where she had sat on the day of his funeral" | Ache |
| 4 | The letter confesses: faked death, debt collectors, five years as nobody | "I did what the sea does best. I disappeared." | Dread (information gap opens) |
| 5 | The letter asks her to keep the secret a little longer | "You are the only one who has ever kept a secret for me" | Warm (trapped warmth) |
| 6 | The postscript-style truth: the last collector died in prison this spring | "The last of them died in prison this spring" | Warm, then held breath |
| 7 | Elara reads it three times, then folds it along old creases | "the creases the paper had learned in five years of waiting" | Ache |
| 8 | The tin box of the dead receives its first living thing | "It had never held anything alive before" | Cold (turning point) |
| 9 | She makes tea; her hands are steady; the tea tastes of nothing | "the way the house had tasted for five years" | Ache (grief compression) |
| 10 | The evening boat; the man who has learned to be nobody | "he stopped, because the pier ended" | Dread |
| 11 | The reunion that was rehearsed and could not survive the journey | "the rehearsing had not survived" | Ache (deflation) |
| 12 | Her claim of unknowing fidelity: "I kept it for five years without knowing" | "That has to count for something" | Warm (the story's emotional peak) |
| 13 | The widow of the debtor was on the boat and knew his face | "She knew my face, Elara" | Dread (reversal trigger) |
| 14 | The sea balances its books; a small boat rides low in the swell | "a single figure at the oars, who was not rowing toward the shore" | Chill (cognitive reversal) |
| 15 | Elara closes the door | "Elara closed the door." | Chill (open close) |

## Beat Structure

The 15 plot points compress into the short-form shape: setup (1-3), promise (4-6), waiting with rising unease (7-9), reunion (10-12), reversal (13-14), open close (15). The emotional weight sits at point 12, not at the reversal: the story chooses the protagonist's choice as its peak, and the mystery as its end.
